# TASKORA — NON-TECHNICAL USER GUIDE

> **Welcome to Taskora!**  
> This guide is designed for **Product Owners, Business Analysts, Project Managers, Team Members, and Stakeholders** who want to use Taskora without dealing with code or complex technical terminology.

---

## 🎯 1. What is Taskora?

**Taskora** is your central workspace for organizing work, tracking project progress, managing software quality, and collaborating with your team. 

Think of Taskora as your digital mission control where you can:
- **Plan projects** and break work down into clear tasks.
- **Track progress in real-time** using visual boards.
- **Test software** to ensure everything works smoothly.
- **Publish releases** and notify team members when features go live.

---

## 🔑 2. Understanding the Basics (Key Concepts)

| Concept | What It Means | Simple Analogy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Organization** | Your company or workspace boundary. | Your office building |
| **Project** | A major product or initiative (e.g., *Website Revamp* or *Mobile App*). | A specific department project |
| **Backlog** | A master list of everything that needs to be done. | Your master to-do list |
| **Sprint** | A short, focused period (usually 1 to 2 weeks) to finish specific tasks. | A 2-week team challenge |
| **Kanban Board** | A visual card board showing what is *Todo*, *In Progress*, *In Review*, and *Done*. | Sticky notes on a whiteboard |
| **Issues / Tasks** | Individual pieces of work (Tasks, Stories, or Bugs). | Action items |
| **QA & Releases** | Quality testing and publishing software updates to users. | Inspection & launch day |

## 🚀 3. How to Use Taskora Step-by-Step

### 📋 Step 1: Navigating Your Workspace

When you log in, you will see the **Left Sidebar**:
1. **Dashboard**: High-level overview of total projects, teams, and active work.
2. **Projects**: View and access all your team's projects.
3. **Issues & Bugs**: View all tasks assigned to you or your project.
4. **Notifications**: In-app alerts for updates, mentions, and assignments.
5. **Activity Log**: Real-time timeline of everything happening in your workspace.
6. **Teams & Members**: Manage who is in your organization and their roles.

---

### ➕ Step 2: Creating a Task or Bug

1. Click on **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Select your project (e.g., *Mobile Application*).
3. Click the **+ New Issue** button at the top right.
4. Fill in the details:
   - **Title**: A clear summary (e.g., *Fix login button alignment on iPhone*).
   - **Type**: Choose **Task**, **Story** (new feature), or **Bug** (issue/defect).
   - **Priority**: Choose **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, or **Urgent**.
   - **Assignee**: Pick the team member responsible.
5. Click **Create Issue**.

---

### 🏃 Step 3: Sprint Planning & Execution

1. Go to your project and click **Sprint Planning Workspace**.
2. Drag or move tasks from your **Product Backlog** into the **Sprint Draft**.
3. Set a **Start Date** and **End Date** for the sprint (e.g., Aug 1 – Aug 15).
4. Click **Start Sprint**.
5. During the sprint, click **Open Kanban Board** to watch tasks move across columns in real-time!

---

### 🎨 Step 4: Using the Visual Kanban Board

The Kanban board lets you track work visually:
- **TODO**: Tasks waiting to be started.
- **IN PROGRESS**: Tasks currently being worked on.
- **IN REVIEW**: Work being reviewed or tested by QA.
- **DONE**: Completed tasks!

Simply drag cards from left to right as work progresses.

---

### 🧪 Step 5: QA Testing & Bug Tracking

For Quality Assurance (QA) managers and testers:
1. Go to the **QA & Test Management** tab inside your project.
2. View **Test Suites** (group of test cases for a feature).
3. Run a **Test Execution**:
   - Mark tests as **PASSED** ✅ or **FAILED** ❌.
   - If a test fails, Taskora automatically prompts you to create a **Bug Ticket** for developers!

---

### 🚀 Step 6: Release Management

When a group of features is ready to launch:
1. Go to **Release Management**.
2. Click **Create Release** (e.g., *Version 1.2.0*).
3. Link the completed Sprints and Test Runs to the release.
4. Review the automated **Health Check** indicator.
5. When ready, click **Publish Release** to notify stakeholders!

## ☀️ 4. Customizing Your Experience (Light & Dark Theme)

Taskora supports both **Dark Theme** 🌙 and **Light Theme** ☀️:
- Click the **Sun / Moon icon** in the top bar or sidebar footer to toggle between modes anytime.
- Your preference is automatically saved!

## 🙋 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: How do I invite a new team member?
> **A**: Go to **Members** in the sidebar $\rightarrow$ Click **+ Add Member** $\rightarrow$ Enter their email address and select their role (e.g., *Developer*, *Tester*, *Project Manager*).

#### Q: Where do I see notifications when someone updates a task?
> **A**: Click the **Bell Icon** 🔔 at the top right of your screen to view your latest notifications.

#### Q: Can I search for a specific task?
> **A**: Go to **Issues & Bugs** and use the search filter by keyword, priority, status, or assignee.
